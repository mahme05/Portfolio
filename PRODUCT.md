# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring teams filling software roles, scanning quickly to decide whether to shortlist Mahmood Abdul Razak. Their job is to confirm what Mahmood has built and is studying, and how to make contact or get the CV.

Other visitors are welcome, but no decision should favour them over a recruiter's quick scan.

## Product Purpose

A public personal portfolio for a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student and software engineer. Success is a recruiter downloading the CV or making contact by email, phone or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A builder across the full range of software, with shipped work to prove it: internal business systems, web apps, Android apps and large-scale data pipelines, taken from first requirement to working software while still a student.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a CV link, LinkedIn or a shared link, on desktop or phone, and usually spend little time. The site is public, and so is its GitHub repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: work index, project detail, experience, credentials, skills, contact, CV download.
- Light and dark modes. The default follows the device setting, and the toggle offers only light and dark.
- Dates use "Mon YYYY" where the month is known.
- Internal employer systems appear only with sample data. No client names, records, declarations or staff identities.
- The customs tool stays a one-line entry, with no product name, screenshot or technical detail.
- Personal details stay limited to email, phone, LinkedIn and GitHub. No date of birth, nationality or residency.
- Doha is mentioned only in experience and studies entries.
- Undecided: B.Tech start and end months, and the start month of the current role.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mahmood Abdul Razak. Role line: software engineer and final-year CSE student at TKM College of Engineering.
- Do not lead with a single specialism such as "full stack developer".
- SBB's design system (Lyne) is adopted as method only: design tokens as the single source of truth, with consistent, accessible components. Do not use SBB's look, logo, name or font.

## Evidence on Hand

- CV: `Mahmood_Abdul_Razak_CV.pdf`.
- Screens: `images/screens/` (live-app captures, and the Aries CRM run on sample data).
- Portrait: `images/portrait.jpg`.
- Certificates with public verification links: Google AI Professional Certificate and Google AI Essentials Specialization.
- Project figures on the site come from the CV and employer certificate.
- Absent, and must not be invented: testimonials, client logos, usage metrics beyond those stated, awards.

## Product Principles

1. Prove range with shipped work, not adjectives.
2. A recruiter should find the CV, the role line and a way to make contact within seconds.
3. Every claim must be defensible in an interview.
4. Employer confidentiality outranks a stronger demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessible components are part of the adopted method: keyboard and screen-reader support, readable contrast in both colour modes, reduced-motion support, and comfortable tap targets on phones.
